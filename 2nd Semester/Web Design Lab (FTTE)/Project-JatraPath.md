# JatraPath - Version 0.1

## Introduction

JatraPath is a travel and tourism website dedicated to exploring the beauty of Bangladesh. The platform helps users discover amazing destinations, plan their journeys, and experience the true spirit of travel across the country.

JatraPath aims to make traveling across Bangladesh simple, enjoyable, and inspiring for everyone. Users can easily access travel information, explore popular tourist spots, and learn about different places, cultures, and experiences.

## Features in Version 0.1

- Explore tourist destinations in Bangladesh
- Basic travel information and guides
- Simple and user-friendly interface
- Destination discovery system
- Responsive website design

## Goal

The goal of JatraPath is to encourage tourism in Bangladesh and help travelers find reliable and inspiring travel information in one place.
